Questions, answered.

Short answers to the things people ask most.

Is QRe really free?

Yes. QRe is free to use, with no account, watermark, or signup. All QR generation happens locally in your browser.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The app does not send your form content to a server. Preferences such as language and theme are stored in localStorage; sensitive fields are not saved.

Which QR code types are supported?

URL, plain text, Wi-Fi, email, phone, SMS, vCard contact, location, calendar event, crypto payments, WhatsApp, Telegram, UPI, and raw custom payloads.

Why does my QR code fail to scan?

Low contrast, a large logo, a missing quiet zone, or a very dense payload can all hurt scanning. Use error correction H with a logo, keep the margin, and test with multiple scanner apps.

Can I use the exported QR code commercially?

Yes. You own the content you generate and can use the exported image in print or digital projects. Make sure you have rights to any logo you upload.
